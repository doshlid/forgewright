Privacy Policy — Forgewright: Blacksmith Sim

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Developer: Doshlid Games (“we”)

Contact: forgewright.games@gmail.com

In short

The game runs on your phone and does not collect your personal data. We show no ads and share no data with third parties. The game does not connect to the internet.

What is stored and where

So that you can continue playing, the game saves on your device:

  • your progress and the items you have forged;
  • settings (language, sound, vibration, whether the tutorial is completed).

This data stays on your device only. We do not receive or see it.

What we do not collect

Your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or gameplay history — the game does not collect or send any of these.

Permissions

  • Vibration — for hammer-strike feedback; you can turn it off in the game settings.

Purchases

If in-game purchases are added, they are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card details; Google handles payments under its Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone settings (Settings → Apps → Forgewright → Storage → Clear data). Since we hold none of your data, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Changes

If online features are added (for example, an arena with other players), we will update this policy in advance and let you know in the game. The date at the top shows when the policy was last changed.

Contact

Privacy questions: forgewright.games@gmail.com
