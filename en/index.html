Forgewright: Blacksmith Sim

Forge swords and armor with real smithing

You inherit an abandoned forge at the edge of a village of master smiths. Light the fire and become the smith everyone talks about. No magic here — just fire, steel and your own hand.

What is in the game

  • Real forgingBellows and working heat, two hammers, spark and magnet tests, quenching in water or oil, tempering by oxide colors, grinding on a wet wheel and honing on a stone.
  • A sword built from partsGuard, grip, pommel and balance. No two blades are alike: shape, temper colors and maker's mark come from your forging.
  • 3D inspectionTurn your finished sword and breastplate in your hands: temper colors, notches and dents from past fights.
  • Armor workshopDish a breastplate on a stump and assemble it with rivets and straps.
  • The shopBuy guards, grips and pommels for assembly, sell spare ore. Spend arena honor on marks and tools.
  • Village ordersVillagers bring orders with conditions. Get to know them — from stranger to friend of the forge.
  • A mine in tiersLantern, oil, backpack. The deeper you go, the richer the ore and the bigger the beasts — a honey badger and a ribbed newt. Fights end in surrender — no one dies.
  • Arena and guild ladderA shadow duel behind a paper screen: only your steel shines. Eight apprentice rivals and a review of every notch.
  • Master's journalWhy steel cracks, what the Curie point is, which animals are endangered — real facts with sources.
  • WorkshopUpgrade the hearth, anvil and bench with your own ore and coins. Guild trials and a wall of your best work.

What's next

The game is in development. Coming up: more kinds of gear — axes, maces, helmets — a leather workshop, the forest and the coast with new animals.
