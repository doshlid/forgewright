Forgewright: Blacksmith Sim

Forge swords and armor with real smithing

You inherit an abandoned forge at the edge of a village of master smiths. Light the fire and become the smith everyone talks about. No magic here — just fire, steel and your own hand.

What is in the game

  • Real forgingBellows and working heat, two hammers, spark and magnet tests, quenching in water or oil, tempering by oxide colors, grinding on a wet wheel.
  • A sword built from partsGuard, grip, pommel and balance. Inspect the finished blade in 3D — no two are alike.
  • Armor workshopDish a breastplate on a stump and assemble it with rivets and straps.
  • Village ordersVillagers bring orders with conditions. Get to know them — from stranger to friend of the forge.
  • Mine expeditionsLantern, oil, backpack. A honey badger and a ribbed newt guard the ore; fights end in surrender — no one dies.
  • Shadow duel arenaA silhouette duel behind a paper screen: only your steel shines. Then a review of every notch and dent.
  • Master's journalWhy steel cracks, what the Curie point is, which animals are endangered — real facts with sources.
  • WorkshopUpgrade the hearth, anvil and bench with your own ore and coins. Guild trials and a wall of your best work.

In development: axes, maces, helmets, a leather workshop, the forest and the coast with new animals.
